=== SHIPPING POLICY ===

We deliver across all of Morocco. Standard delivery: 2–5 business days.

Fast delivery cities (2–3 days): Casablanca, Rabat, Salé, Marrakech, Fès, Meknès, Agadir, Tanger, Oujda, Kenitra, Tétouan, Nador.

Remote areas (Laâyoune, Dakhla, Errachidia, Zagora): 5–7 business days.

Shipping is FREE on orders above 400 MAD.
Orders below 400 MAD: flat fee of 35 MAD.

You will receive an SMS with a tracking link once your order is dispatched.

⚠️ Eid Delivery Notice: High volume during Eid season — order by May 28 to guarantee Eid delivery. We cannot guarantee delivery for orders placed after that date.

We ship within Morocco only. No international shipping currently.


=== CASH ON DELIVERY (COD) ===

COD is available nationwide. Pay the delivery agent in cash when your order arrives.

Please have the exact amount ready — delivery agents may not carry change.
COD orders require a reachable phone number — the agent will call before arriving.

If the agent cannot reach you after 2 attempts, the order is returned and a 35 MAD reshipping fee applies.

COD is not available for orders above 2000 MAD — card payment required.


=== RETURNS & REFUNDS ===

Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery.
Items must be unused, unwashed, with original tags attached.

To return: WhatsApp us your order number and reason → we arrange pickup → refund processed within 5–7 business days after inspection.

Refund methods:
- Card payments → refunded to original card.
- COD orders → bank transfer or store credit.

Cannot be returned:
- Underwear and intimate apparel.
- Items marked "Non-retournable" (final sale).
- Customized or personalized pieces.

Wrong item or defect? Send us a photo on WhatsApp — we replace at no cost.


=== EXCHANGES ===

Exchanges accepted within 14 days for a different size or color, subject to availability.
Exchange shipping is free if the original item was delivered correctly.
If the replacement costs more, you pay the difference. If less, we issue store credit.


=== PAYMENT METHODS ===

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===

Q: Can I order a specific Eid outfit in time?
A: Yes, if you order before May 28. After that we cannot guarantee Eid delivery due to high demand.

Q: Can I get a mother-daughter matching outfit?
A: Yes! The Takchita Rania and Kaftan Mini Lina are available in matching colors. Very popular this Eid season.

Q: I don't know my size — what do I do?
A: Send your measurements (bust, waist, hips in cm) on WhatsApp and we'll tell you exactly which size to order.

Q: Can I order by WhatsApp?
A: Yes. Send the product name, color, size, and your delivery address. We'll confirm and process manually.

Q: Do you do gift wrapping?
A: Yes, 20 MAD extra. Add a note at checkout with the recipient's name for a handwritten card.

Q: I ordered the wrong size — can I change it?
A: Contact us within 1 hour of ordering. After that, you'll need to exchange after delivery.

Q: Are the colors in the photos accurate?
A: We try to keep photos accurate, but slight variation is possible due to screen settings. For Eid pieces specifically, if the color is important, ask us to send a real photo on WhatsApp.
