=== BATTERY, RANGE & CHARGING FAQ ===

Q: How long does the battery last (lifespan, not per-charge)?
A: All batteries are rated for 500–800 full charge cycles (roughly 2–3 years of normal daily use) before noticeable capacity loss.

Q: Can I ride in the rain?
A: Our scooters and e-bikes are splash-resistant (IP54) but not fully waterproof. Avoid deep puddles and heavy rain. We sell a rain cover for extra protection.

Q: Range advertised vs. real range?
A: Advertised range is measured under ideal conditions (flat road, 70kg rider, eco mode). Expect 15-25% less in real city conditions depending on rider weight, terrain, and speed mode.

Q: Can the battery be swapped/upgraded?
A: E-bikes have removable batteries — upgrades and spares available. Scooter batteries are internal on most models except the MaxSpeed 8, which supports a spare battery pack.

Q: What if the battery stops holding charge?
A: Covered under warranty if within the warranty period and not due to physical damage or water exposure. Contact us with your order number.

=== RETURNS & WARRANTY ===

Returns accepted within 7 days of delivery if unused, in original packaging, with no signs of riding (battery must show zero or near-zero usage cycles).
Once a scooter/e-bike has been ridden, it is no longer eligible for a full return — only warranty repair applies.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects in motor, battery, and frame (see individual product warranty periods).
Warranty does NOT cover: water damage, crash/impact damage, unauthorized repairs, normal battery capacity degradation over time.

To claim warranty: WhatsApp us your order number, a description of the issue, and a photo/video if possible.
Minor repairs (tires, brakes, cables) can often be handled by any local bike/scooter repair shop — ask us first, we may cover the cost if it's a warranty issue.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===

Q: Do I need a license to ride an electric scooter/bike in Morocco?
A: No license is required for personal-use electric scooters/bikes under current regulations. We recommend wearing a helmet and following local traffic rules.

Q: Does it come assembled?
A: Scooters arrive mostly assembled — just unfold and charge. E-bikes require basic assembly (handlebars, pedals, front wheel) — instructions included, takes about 15-20 minutes. We can recommend a local shop if you'd prefer professional assembly.

Q: Which model is best for daily commuting under 10km?
A: Urban X7 (scooter) or E-Bike City Comfort — both are efficient for short daily trips and easier on the budget.

Q: Which model is best for delivery work?
A: MaxSpeed 8 Off-Road (scooter) or Cargo Delivery Pro (e-bike) — built for higher daily mileage and heavier loads.

Q: Can I test ride before buying?
A: Yes, at our Casablanca showroom (Quartier Gauthier). WhatsApp us to book a time slot.

Q: Do you offer installment payments?
A: Not directly, but our CMI partner supports installment plans on eligible cards — ask your bank about "3x sans frais" (3x no-fee installments) eligibility.
